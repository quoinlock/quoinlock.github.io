Watch your books everywhere they appear

Add your ISBNs once. Every day Quoinlock checks the record of truth, the retailers and the libraries, and tells you what changed.

Daily Listing Checks

Price, availability and metadata from Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Bookshop.org, Google Books and up to 44 retail, independent and library sources, checked every day.

Official & Distribution Data

See your official ISBN record (38+ fields, from dimensions to BISAC subjects) side by side with Ingram distribution data and what retailers actually show.

Change Alerts

Know when a price moves, a listing appears or disappears, your official record changes, or the Library of Congress assigns your LCCN. Delivered instantly or as a daily or weekly digest.

See your books the way libraries do

Library data is metadata too. Quoinlock brings the Library of Congress and the world's research libraries into the same view as your retail listings.

Library of Congress & MARC

Your LoC MARC record and LCCN, refreshed daily, with decoded cataloging-agency codes, MARCXML download and side-by-side MARC comparison between any two ISBNs.

Library Catalog Lookup

Query the catalogs of roughly 50 national, state, research and university libraries to see exactly how each one has catalogued any ISBN.

LC Call Number Calculator

Estimate a Library of Congress call number from LC's own classification data, with an author Cutter fitted against LC's shelflist.

Fix it with professional cataloging tools

Tools that used to need a cataloger, a consultant or an expensive desktop app, built on the same official and Library of Congress data.

PCIP Generator

Produce a Publisher's Cataloging-in-Publication block for your copyright page, with subject headings, LC and Dewey classification and author dates, in modern or legacy card format.

ONIX Generator

Turn an ISBN into a schema-valid ONIX 2.1, 3.0 or 3.1 record, built from the official ISBN record and enriched from the Library of Congress, with per-field provenance and full editing.

Author Identity

Find your authors' ISNI, LC Name Authority and ORCID identifiers across VIAF, ISNI, the Library of Congress, ORCID and Wikidata, with confidence levels and next steps when there's no match.
